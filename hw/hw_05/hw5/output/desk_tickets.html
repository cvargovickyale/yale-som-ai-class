Why the $840 wasn't spent: keeping cash available for reprints and emergencies. Paying invoice 501 and the rent together would have taken checking from $3,400 to $160, before the team even knew what the tee reprint would cost. Accounting's call, in its own words: "after the $2,400 rent due in two days only $160 remains; the reprint quantity/cost is unconfirmed, so confirm that cost before committing." The Boss agreed: "offer available alternatives rather than make an uncertain commitment." Holding the $840 kept a $1,000 cushion for reprints or anything unexpected, and Tauhid still got a real option (M, L or XL in stock today). The trade-off: invoice 501 stays overdue, and Bulldog Print Co won't ship a reprint until it's paid. It's flagged on the board's overdue ticker so it isn't forgotten. (It differs from the P6 plan, which assumed paying it first. No payment request was created, so the board had nothing to approve.)
